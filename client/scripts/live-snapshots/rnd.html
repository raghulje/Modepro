Research and Development

Our R&D centre located at Mumbai provides process research for API intermediates.
Our team of researchers have developed several novel processes for producing intermediates of well-known API’s. Other activities include analytical process development and IP analysis.

Major Activities:
  • Route screening
  • Process Development
  • Scale up
  • Technology Transfer
  • Synthesizing Impurities
  • Contract Research
